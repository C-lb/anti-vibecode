Flex and grid children need min-width:0, or a long child refuses to shrink and pushes whatever sits next to it out of the box.

Measure
DoCapped near 68 characters

Running text stops around 68 characters. The eye finds the start of the next line without hunting, which is what makes a long block feel calm rather than heavy.

Don'tFull-width line, no cap

Running text that runs the whole width of a wide container forces the eye to travel back across the full column, and by the third or fourth line the reader starts losing their place no matter how generous the leading is.

Section 4

Section 5

Icons

One family, stroke-matched, sized to the text. Interface icons fall back Phosphor or Feather, then Ionicons, then Remix Icon. Logos fall back Ionicons logo-*, then Simple Icons or Remix Icon.

Interface set, stroke 2, inline SVG

Same stroke width, same corner rounding, all at 1em so they sit on the text baseline. A row of icons should look stamped from one die.

Logos, Ionicons logo-* set
When Ionicons has no mark, Simple Icons

Brand marks are Ionicons logo-* first, then Simple Icons or Remix Icon for anything Ionicons lacks. Never a webfont. Both rows here load from a CDN, so they need a connection. In a real project, inline the SVGs you actually use.
